02 · Today’s options

A black box, a workshop — or a guess.

The dashboard number

Computed by the car’s own software. One number — no range, no evidence, nothing a buyer can check.

UnverifiableNo confidence range

Special tools & workshops

Diagnostic equipment, an appointment, sometimes a full controlled charge–discharge. Slow, costly, hard to repeat for every sale.

TimeCostEquipment

A guess from age & km

Ignores how the battery was actually used and charged. Two cars with the same age and mileage can have very different batteries.

ImpreciseEasy to dispute
Uncertainty gets priced in — and everyone pays for it.
